# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- **Every choice has a source.** Each major design choice now names where it came from: the brief, the subject, the existing system, or a requirement. A choice with no source is treated as a default. (`principles.md`, design note in `SKILL.md`)
- **Section filter.** Every section must explain, prove, deepen or convert, or it's cut. (`composition.md`)
- **Brand test.** On Persuade pages, hide the logo and nav: if the first screen could belong to a competitor, the identity isn't in it yet. (`composition.md`, `review.md`)
- **Worst-case content.** Components are checked against long, empty, huge and translated content and missing images. (`interaction.md`, `review.md`)
- **Evidence honesty in reviews.** Never claim to have looked at, measured or tested something without evidence; counts and values come from the page or files, not memory. (`review.md`)
- **User-reported sizes.** If the user reported a problem at a specific size, that size is rendered before and after the fix. (`review.md`)
- **DESIGN.md Don'ts become checks** during review. (`review.md`)
- **More generic defaults caught:** hairline border plus wide soft shadow, middot and dash meta labels, oversized headlines with extreme negative letter-spacing, numbered markers on non-sequences, the default "broadsheet" look, arrows on every call to action, zoom-on-hover for every image. (`tells.md`)
